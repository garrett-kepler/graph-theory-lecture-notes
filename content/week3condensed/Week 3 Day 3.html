The main object in pandas is the pandas dataframe which is similar to a spreadsheet or table. Moreover, we can convert the dataframe into whatever format we like.

For example, consider the following table.

Name

Age

Major

Alice

20

Mathematics

Bob

22

Physics

Charlie

19

Biology

If I wanted to convert this into a dataframe, I could do the following:

Name = ['Alice', 'Bob', 'Charlie']
Age = [20, 22, 19]
Major = ['Mathematics', 'Physics', 'Biology']

dataframe = pd.DataFrame({
    'Name': Name,
    'Age': Age,
    'Major': Major
})
dataframe
Name Age Major
0 Alice 20 Mathematics
1 Bob 22 Physics
2 Charlie 19 Biology
